The game in a nutshell

Unknown Adventure is a cooperative isometric action RPG for one to four players, built one small, complete, playable step at a time. You are looking at Stage 1: a short Courier Route that tests movement, the camera, a readable level and sharing things between players: collect three items, carry them through a hazard field and hand them in together. There is no combat yet.

Where the project is

The game grows in stages. Each one ends in something you can play and give feedback on, and asks one main question.

#What you can playMain question
0Cross-platform multiplayer sandboxCan the chosen technology support the intended experience?Done
1Courier-style exploration challengeIs moving and interacting enjoyable and readable?Now
2Small cooperative defence encounterIs the basic fight worth replaying?Next
3Three-room micro-dungeonDo equipment and abilities create meaningful choices?Later
4One polished, representative adventureDoes the complete experience work as a small RPG?Later
5Connected expeditions with lasting progressDoes progression keep players interested beyond one session?Later
6Steam test builds and friend sessionsCan players find, join and play reliably?Later
7Release candidateIs the game coherent, stable and supportable?Later

Stage 1 today: the Courier Route you can play now: items to pick up and carry, hazards, checkpoints, a shared objective and timer, and menus that work with a controller. Stage 0 (the networking foundation) is done. Stage 1 closes after friends play it and the findings are reviewed.

Parts of the game

Each part has its own section on the mechanics page, with screenshots, its history and its bugs.

  • Sessions: Solo, Host, Join, Server v0.1.0
    Play alone, host friends, join by address, or run a server.
  • Recent hosts v0.2.2
    The last three hosts you joined, two clicks away.
  • Host addresses v0.2.1
    Every address friends can use, with Copy buttons.
  • Movement and camera v0.1.0
    Instant, camera-relative movement that stays in sync.
  • Controls and devices v0.1.0
    Keyboard, mouse and gamepad; hints always on screen.
  • Items, carrying and delivery v0.2.0
    Pick up, carry one at a time, drop or hand in; exactly one holder.
  • The shared objective and the timer v0.3.0
    Hand in three items together; everyone sees the same progress.
  • Health, hazards and falling v0.3.0
    Red strips hurt; fall and you come back at a checkpoint.
  • Checkpoints and starting a run again v0.3.0
    Progress moves forward; start over without leaving.
  • Menus, HUD and the status bar v0.3.0
    Everything works with a controller; the status never goes stale.
  • The pulse v0.2.0
    An ability whose cooldown the server keeps.
  • Joining late and starting over v0.1.0
    Join a running game; end it and nothing is left behind.
  • Updates and versions v0.1.0
    Stay current, go back, and know who can play together.
