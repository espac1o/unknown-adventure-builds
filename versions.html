Version history

Versions

What each version added, fixed and left open, with a link to its release. Newest first.

Everyone playing together must be on exactly the same version. To update, open the launcher and press Enter (see the user guide); older versions can be installed again from the menu.

v0.3.25

2026-11-10Latest

A catch resets your throw and gives a second of fast charging, statuses show under the health bar, other players fade out and in properly when they respawn, and the recording now says when a window was in the background.

What's new
  • Catching a ball makes your throw ready at once. For 1 second after, a throw you start charges twice as fast, and holding it too long costs half the penalty.
  • Under the health bar there are now statuses: positive ones (carrying a ball, speed boost, quick throw) in one row, negative ones (slowed while charging, stunned) in the row below, each with a radial timer. They replace the "Carrying" text.
  • A throw you made just before dying or being stunned is accepted for a full round trip of your connection, not one way.
  • On other screens a player who died is hidden from the moment they are down until they stand on their respawn point, and then appears with the fade.
  • The recording marks every second in which the window of the game was in the background (it is held to 20 frames a second then), so a low frame rate is not mistaken for a fault.
Fixed
  • A throw let go of just before a death was sometimes undone for the thrower: the ball flew a few metres and then went back.
  • On other screens a dead player became see-through, was drawn again where they died and then jumped to the respawn point.
Under the hood

Interaction 0.8.6.

Known limits

Not yet explained (perhaps the low-energy profile): the Deck dropped to 76 frames a second once without anything happening, and the health bar of the Deck seemed to lag a few times (the server's damage steps were even).

v0.3.24

2026-11-10Latest

A throw you made just before dying or being stunned now counts, a ball you threw no longer shifts after it lands, and other players are hidden between their death and their return.

What's new

  • On other screens a player who was knocked out stays out of sight from the end of the fade until they stand on their respawn point, instead of showing for a moment on the place of their death.
  • The ball hangs a little lower and closer to the body while you charge a throw, instead of sinking almost to the ground.
  • Other players no longer turn on a few extra ticks when they stop, which made them swing back and forth.
Fixed
  • The ball you threw shifted a few seconds after it landed (and, after a hit, toward the player who was hit), only on your own screen.
  • A player who died was drawn for a moment, alive, on the place of their death on the other screen, and seemed to walk before being put on the respawn point.
Under the hood

Interaction 0.8.5.

Known limits

Turning back and forth when another player stops is only partly understood (this release removes one cause); the recording now counts it.

v0.3.13

2026-11-08Latest

A dedicated server for everyone (one command to run it, a menu entry to join it), a ring in the corner while your session data is being sent, and a question before you quit with data still on its way.

What's new
  • Dedicated server. The Linux build runs without a screen on a machine of its own. A session starts when the first player joins and ends when the last one leaves; the server then sends its recording and waits for the next player. Under the hood

Analytics 2.1.0. Server session rules in RunRules.sessionStep; the server writes a status file and obeys command files, which is how gamehost (over ssh) controls it. The Linux player crashes at start with -nographics, so the server runs it on a virtual display with the drawing turned down.

Known limits

v0.3.8

2026-11-07Latest

The throw rules are stricter and clearer, a hit pushes the player and drops the ball where it hit, coming back starts with Ready? and Go!, runs restart after a countdown, and the game no longer works the machine at full load for nothing.

What's new
  • Throw rules. The throw does nothing while it recharges. Let go before a fifth of the charge (0.4 seconds) and nothing is thrown; the ball goes back as it was. The charge circle is yellow until letting go would throw, then green. E (A) puts the ball down again.

  • The drawn body glided from the death point after a respawn, because the smoothing of the drawn body had no limit on a jump. It now follows at once.

Interaction 0.4.0. Tests: 197 unit and 24 play-mode.

The numbers (the fifth of the charge, 1 second of boost, the push of 0.6 metres, one second each of Ready? and Go!) are first guesses. Respawn timing between a client and the server was still off in this version; see 0.3.9.

v0.3.7

2026-11-07Latest

Throwing is charged and has consequences: a ball that hits a player stuns them, and a player who is quick enough catches it. Menus come back to a controller after a click, and a player who respawns is shown on the respawn point at once.

What's new
  • Charged throw (R / RB). A tap lets go of the ball (it keeps your speed); this replaces the Drop button, and E only picks up now. Hold the button to charge, up to 2 seconds, and let go to throw: the longer you held, the harder it flies. While you charge you walk slower, your colour darkens a little and the ball sinks and is drawn back, for everyone to see.
  • Hit. A thrown ball that reaches another player stuns them for 1 second and gives the thrower a speed boost as strong as the charge (up to +60% for 3 seconds).
  • Catch (E / A). Press it just before the ball arrives (up to 0.4 seconds) and you catch it and get the boost instead. It can be used again after 1.2 seconds. The skill bar has a Catch slot.
  • Menus: after a click outside the buttons, an arrow, WASD, the d-pad or the stick selects an item again.
Fixed
  • Menu focus lost after a click. A click or touch outside the buttons cleared the selection, and then no key or stick did anything. The next navigation press now selects the item that was selected last (or the top one), without also moving on.
  • Respawn on your own screen. Your character stayed on the place where you died until the server's message that you were back arrived, and kept walking there if you held a direction. The server now sends the respawn point and tick ahead, and your own predicted character is placed there on that tick. In the automatic test your character reaches the respawn point 0.01 to 0.02 seconds after the server does, with 30 to 250 ms of delay (it took 0.21 to 0.45 seconds before).
  • The ball stayed in the thrower's hands until the server answered. It now leaves the hand at once on the thrower's screen on a flight of its own and hands over to the server's ball when both have stopped; the server moves the ball on by the time the request took. With 40 ms of delay and 3% loss the ball is a metre from the hand 81 ms after letting go.
Under the hood

Interaction 0.3.0, Units 3.2.0, GameUI 0.1.2, Objectives 0.2.0, IsoCamera 0.2.0. The stun and the boost are replicated as ticks so the owner's prediction knows when they end; the charge is part of the predicted input, so the server walks as the owner does. Tests: 195 unit, 24 play-mode (including new scenarios for the respawn timing, the charge, the hit, the stun, the boost and the catch) and 74 installer checks.

Known limits

Nobody but the developer has played it yet. The numbers (charge time, speeds, 1 second of stun, +60% boost, catch window of 0.4 seconds and cooldown of 1.2) are first guesses for the playtest: tell us if the stun is too long, the boost too strong or the catch too hard or too easy. The ball does not yet knock the stunned player's own ball out of their hands.

v0.3.5

2026-11-07Latest

A dropped or thrown ball now moves evenly on the other players' screens, not only on the host's.

What's new
  • Nothing new to play: this build fixes how a falling, rolling or thrown ball looks to a player who is not the host.

The green pad counts the balls lying on it, balls roll and can be thrown, checkpoints have flags, and a few bugs from the third playtest are fixed. The launcher downloads faster.

  • The pad counts, you decide when to drop. There is no Deliver press any more: a ball that lies on the green pad counts, and picking it up again counts it out, so balls can be put in and taken out. A held ball never counts.
  • Balls are physical. A dropped ball falls, bounces and rolls to a stop; dropped while you walk it keeps half your speed and rolls on.
  • Throwing. Hold Interact for half a second to throw (about 6 metres the way you face); a tap only drops. The throw recharges for 5 seconds; the hint shows it.
  • Checkpoint flags. Each checkpoint has a flag and a border: grey and low ahead, green and raised when it is the active one, dark green once passed.
  • A ball that leaves the map goes back to where it started.
Fixed
  • The unit flickered between two places after a restart or revive while other players moved (only on your own screen). FishNet clients kept their own history of past states, which still held the old position. They no longer do.
  • A checkpoint was reached from inside the red strips. It used a round area around its middle that reached into them. It is now reached by stepping onto its pad.
  • The Join screen could not be used with a controller: the address field kept the controller to itself. Up, down, the stick or B now leave it.
  • A dead unit slows down instead of stopping at once.
Under the hood

Interaction 0.2.0, GameUI 0.1.1, Objectives 0.2.0, IsoCamera 0.2.0. The launcher fetches the archive in four parts at once (GitHub limits each connection: 32 MB took 18 seconds instead of about three minutes in a measurement), prints how long the download, the check and the unpacking took, and, on a Steam Deck in Gaming Mode, shows a progress window when zenity is installed. Automatic tests: 176 unit tests, 19 play-mode tests and 74 installer checks.

Known limits

Not seen on a screen yet by anyone but the developer: the look of the thrown ball, the hold bar and the checkpoint flags. The Windows launcher change (parallel download) has not been run on Windows, and the Steam Deck progress window has not been seen on a Deck. Players walk through balls and cannot kick them.
