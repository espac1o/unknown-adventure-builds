Playtest guide

Thank you for testing. This page tells you what to install, what to try and what to tell me.

About 20 minutes. You need the game on your computer or Steam Deck, and ideally one or two friends. Please do not read the Mechanics page first: one of the questions is whether a new player understands the game without any explanation.

What this test is about

Version 0.3.23 has one short route, Courier Route: collect three items, carry them through a hazard field and put them down on the green pad together. It is built to answer three questions:

  • Where did movement or the camera get in the way of what you wanted to do?
  • Were the hazards and the places you could walk clear?
  • Did playing together help, or did you just share the room?

There is no combat and no art yet: the level is made of coloured blocks on purpose, so we can judge whether the space is readable.

Before you start

  1. Install the game with the user guide (Mac, Steam Deck or Windows). Everyone in a session must be on exactly version 0.3.13; it is written at the top of the main menu.
  2. Pick how you will play: keyboard and mouse, or a controller. If you can, try the controller for one whole round, menus included.
  3. For playing together, one person chooses Host a game and, in the pause menu (Esc or Start), Invite friends shows the addresses to send. The others choose Join a game.

What to do

  1. Alone, without help (about 5 minutes). Choose Solo and try to work out what to do. Do not read anything. Notice every moment you were unsure what to do next, and what finally told you.
  2. Together (about 10 minutes). One hosts, the others join. Complete the route together at least twice. After the first time, the host presses Play again (or Restart run in the pause menu) and you go again without anyone leaving.
  3. Try to break it (about 5 minutes). Fall into the red strips with an item. Drop an item in odd places. Leave the session and join again in the middle of a run. Grab the same item at the same time. Try to pick things up through a wall (it should not work). Drop balls on the green pad and take them out again, throw one (hold the Throw button, R or RB, for half a second; let go earlier and nothing happens), watch the skills at the bottom left, where the ball lands and whether it moves evenly when you watch another player throw it, and throw one off the edge of the map. Press buttons in the menus with a controller only. Tell us what happened.

Known limits

  • Nobody but the developer has played this route yet; the damage of the red strips (25 a second), the pick-up reach and the camera are first guesses.
  • There is no combat, no sound and no art: that is on purpose for now.
  • Typing an address needs a keyboard; with a controller use the list of recent hosts.
  • How the menus feel on the Steam Deck in Gaming Mode, and the Windows launcher, have had little testing.
  • Nothing is saved after a run: no best times.
